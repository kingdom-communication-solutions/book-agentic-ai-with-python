# Campus Café — synthetic teaching data

These files describe a fictional university café, not a real company or university. They cover 90 dates from 5 January to 4 April 2026. Currency is EUR.

| File | Rows | Content |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| sales.csv | 450 | Daily quantities, sale prices and revenue for five products |
| weather.csv | 90 | Daily temperature, rainfall and conditions |
| academic_calendar.csv | 90 | Daily academic-calendar context |
| campus_events.csv | 6 | Recorded fictional campus events |
| promotions.csv | 13 | Recorded product promotions |

See `data_dictionary.md` for column names, types and record keys. Dates absent from the sparse events or promotions files have no corresponding generated record. Sales prices already reflect recorded discounts. These synthetic records support classroom analysis; they do not establish real-world causal effects.
